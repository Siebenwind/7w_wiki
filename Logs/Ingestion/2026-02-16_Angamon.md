# Ingestion Report: Angamon

## Executive Summary
Re-ingestion of the specific "Angamon" canon source. Updated his status in the current lore timeline (post-Götterwandel) and his divine origin.

## Extracted Lore

### Origin & Biography
- **Parentage**: Angamon is the geächtete son of **Vitama** and **Bellum**.
- **Exile**: Banished to the 2nd sphere by the Four (Sahor).

### Political Status (v2.7)
- **Götterwandel**: Since the legitimization by Zälat **Argion**, the Angamon cult is no longer considered illegal/heresy. It is now part of the recognized spectrum of belief (Skrupellosigkeit, Right of the stronger).

### Motives
- **Conquest vs Destruction**: Unlike demons, Angamon wants to conquer and rule **Tare**, not destroy it. He uses the demons as tools but does not share their nihilistic goals.

### Trivia
- **Curse**: High-level servants of Angamon suffer from the "Fluch der Viere," making it extremely difficult to conceive healthy children.
