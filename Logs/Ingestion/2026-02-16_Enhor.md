## Extracted Lore

### General Theological Principles
- **Ambivalence**: Every lord possesses both life-giving and destructive aspects.
- **Regulatory Cycle**: Ignis and Xan exist in a state of "hatred-love," where they regulate each other's extremes to maintain a habitable temperature on Tare.
- **Neutrality**: The Enhor remain neutral in the disputes of the Sahor (the Five) unless their material interests are threatened.

- **Rien & Tare**: Created the Elves on the island of **Lothorien**.
- **Xan**: Patron of healers and source of life; executor of the *Winter di Luce* ritual.
- **Ventus**: Enabled the path to Siebenwind by withdrawing his storms.
