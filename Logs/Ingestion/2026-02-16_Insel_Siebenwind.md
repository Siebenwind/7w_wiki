## Executive Summary
Re-ingestion of the "Die Insel Siebenwind" canon document. Aligned the geographic and political state of the island with the latest canonical standard, particularly regarding the ruined status of major settlements and the racial composition of the Inselrat.

## Extracted Lore

### Political Structure
- **Barony**: Siebenwind is a Barony under **Großkönig Hilgorad I.** and **Königin Brynn I.**
- **Administration**: Managed by a **Kanzler**, who is elected by the **Inselrat**.
- **Inselrat**: Composed of representatives from the Elves, Dwarves, Halflings, Nortraven, and the **Kaz'la** (Orcs). This ensures a voice for non-Galadonian residents.

### Geographical Status (Current)
- **Ruins**: **Falkensee**, **Südfall**, and **Kesselklamm** (as well as the "Ödland" settlements) are currently in ruins due to conflicts with the forces of [[Angamon]].
- **Resilience**: Despite the destruction, the island remains a "Schicksalsinsel" (Island of Fate) attracting those seeking destiny or refuge.
