# Ingestion Report: Auenelfen

## Executive Summary
Re-ingestion of the extensive "Auenelfen" canon document. Upgraded the Auenelfen lore to v2.7 standard, including physiology, origins, and communal social structures.

## Extracted Lore

### Physiology & Life Cycle
- **Size**: 1.80 m to 2.20 m.
- **Eye Colors**: Deep blue or vibrant green.
- **Life Span**: No natural death; death is voluntary upon "completion" of life.
- **Magie**: Universal but moderate magic capacity.

### History & Origins
- **Creation**: 2nd Age of Tare by the Enhor (Elemental Lords).
- **Separation**: Natural catastrophe (Dämmerungs-Zyklus) forced them out of the forests.
- **Miandrell**: Primeval village and holy site in Western Auren.

### Society
- **Communalism**: Shared property system (Gemeinschaftskiste) in villages like [[Avindhrell]].
- **Hierarchy**: Elders' Council (Dorfrat); no rigid class system.
- **Protectors**: **Elendur inn Amrai** (Protectors of the Auen).
- **Taboos**: Intense discomfort in caves/underground spaces.
